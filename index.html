Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about building your resume with ResumeCraft.

Is ResumeCraft completely free?

Yes! ResumeCraft is 100% free with no account registration, hidden fees, or subscriptions required.

Are these resumes really ATS-friendly?

Yes. All 4 templates are designed using standard single-column or clean two-column structures, readable standard fonts, and semantic headers that ATS parsers can read seamlessly.

Is my personal information safe?

Absolutely. ResumeCraft runs 100% client-side in your web browser. None of your personal data is sent to external servers. It stays safely in your browser's local storage.

How do I download my resume as PDF?

Click the "Download PDF" button in the builder action toolbar. Our client-side rendering engine converts your live preview directly into an A4 PDF document.
